Privacy Policy for Glass Widgets

Application: Glass Widgets
Developer: Jovii Studio / Bikram Pratap Singh
Effective Date: October 30, 2026
Public Policy URL: https://bikrampratapsingh.github.io/glass-widgets/

On-device first: Glass Widgets has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your calendar, steps, screen time, music, contacts, photos and reminders are used only on your phone to draw your widgets.

Glass Widgets ("the app") is an Android app of frosted glass home-screen widgets, made by Jovii Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be short and honest: the app has no account, no sign-in and no server of our own, and we do not sell your data.

1. Summary

  • Your calendar events, steps, screen time, music, contacts, photos and reminders are used only on your phone to draw your widgets. We never receive them.
  • The app connects to a few services to work: Open-Meteo (weather and air quality), Cloudflare (speed tests you start), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (update checks).
  • Every permission is optional. If you decline one, only the widgets that need it stop showing that information.

2. Information used on your device

The app asks for the following access only when you use a widget that needs it. This information is processed on your phone and is not sent to us or to anyone else, except as described in section 3.

AccessWhat it is used for
Calendar (read only)Shows your events on the calendar widgets and finds upcoming flights in your calendar for the travel widgets.
Approximate locationFinds your city for weather and air quality. The app uses approximate (city-level) location only, never precise location, and does not request background location access. You can search for a city instead.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth)Shows the battery level of your connected earbuds and other Bluetooth devices.
Physical activityCounts your steps for the fitness widgets, using the phone's step sensor.
Usage accessShows your screen time and most-used apps.
Notification accessReads only the track that is playing (title, artist, artwork, play state) for the music widgets. Other notifications are ignored and never stored.
Do Not Disturb accessLets the Silent and Do Not Disturb widgets switch modes. Asked only when you tap one of them.
Modify system settingsLets the Quick Settings widgets change brightness, auto-rotate and screen timeout only when you tap them.
Contacts you pickThe contacts widgets use the system contact picker, one person at a time. The app saves only the name and number of the people you choose. It cannot read your address book.
Photos you pickThe photo widgets use the system photo picker. The app keeps a small copy of each photo you choose, inside its own storage. It cannot browse your gallery.
Set wallpaper, set alarm, vibrateSets a wallpaper you choose, starts a timer in your clock app from the timer widget, and gives a short vibration when you switch to Vibrate mode.

Things you create in the app, such as reminders, widget styles, counters and favourites, are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

3. Information shared with third parties

Open-Meteo (weather and air quality). To fetch the forecast, the app sends the coordinates of your chosen or approximate location to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Like any website, Open-Meteo receives your IP address. No other information is sent. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information.

Cloudflare (speed tests). Speed tests run only when you tap the speed test widget. They download and upload test data to Cloudflare's speed test servers (speed.cloudflare.com), which receive your IP address. Results are kept only on your phone. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Google AdMob (ads). The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that unlock premium widgets. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Google Play (update checks). When you tap "Check for updates", the app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except as described above.

4. Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up the app's settings and saved data (such as widget styles, reminders, unlocked widgets, and the contacts and photos you picked for widgets) to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google and encrypted according to your device's backup settings. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

5. Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you remove it. Removing a widget deletes its saved style and any photo only it used. You can delete all of the app's data at any time by clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Glass Widgets → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; to remove data held by AdMob, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

6. Security

All network requests made by the app use encrypted HTTPS connections. Data stored by the app is kept in its private storage, which other apps cannot read.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your choices

  • Grant or revoke any permission at any time in your phone's Settings → Apps → Glass Widgets → Permissions (Usage, Notification, Do Not Disturb and Modify system settings access are under Special app access).
  • Choose a city instead of sharing your location.
  • Change your ad consent in the app's Settings → Ad privacy, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
