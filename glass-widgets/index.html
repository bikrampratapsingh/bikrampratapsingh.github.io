Privacy Policy for Glass Widgets

Application: Glass Widgets
Developer: Jovii Studio / Bikram Pratap Singh
Effective Date: October 2026
Public Policy URL: https://bikrampratapsingh.github.io/glass-widgets/

100% On-Device Personalization: Glass Widgets renders all translucent widgets, battery indicators, and weather/clock graphics locally on your device. We do not operate external tracking servers or collect personal identifiers.

1. Introduction

Glass Widgets ("we", "our", or "the app") provides customizable frosted-glass and Material You widgets for Android homescreens. We are committed to transparency and respect user privacy at every level.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Glass Widgets operates without user registration or cloud accounts. We do not harvest, monetize, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, contact books, or personal media files.

  • Widget Customizations: Layout configurations, style choices, and font selections are stored exclusively on your device in secure local preferences.
  • System Metrics: Telemetry such as battery levels, network status, or time are read purely in memory to refresh widget views on your homescreen.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development, Glass Widgets may display digital ads provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob complies with Google Play policies and may use anonymized device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) solely for frequency capping and fraud detection.
