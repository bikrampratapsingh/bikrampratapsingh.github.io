Style

Light or dark glass, frost from 0 to 100, and a choice of fonts, including a dot-matrix style.

Copies

Wallpapers

A curated set of originals: abstract, AMOLED, art, bikes, city, minimal, mountains, nature, ocean and space. Set one in a tap on your home screen, lock screen or both.

Private by design.

Your calendar events, steps, screen time, reminders and photos stay on your phone, and widgets only ask for access when you use a feature that needs it.
